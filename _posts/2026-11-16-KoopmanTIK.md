A Koopman controller can stabilize its learned model and still fail on the robot. Tangent-Invariant Koopman (TIK) makes the learned model match the robot's linearization at the target. On four simulated unstable benchmarks, all 40 TIK controllers stabilize the nonlinear plant, and all 40 standard Koopman controllers do not.
